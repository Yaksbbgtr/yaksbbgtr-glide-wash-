Glide Wash Pass
Concept redesign

The wash pass, rebuilt to feel like iOS.

A tappable prototype. Start by creating an account (or tap Sign in), then click through the tabs, tap the pass to enlarge the code, open a location, or switch plans.

  • Sign-up in six short steps: name, contact, text-code check, car, plan and card.
  • Pass first. The scan code opens instantly and goes full-screen white for the scanner.
  • Four clear tabs with labels, where the old app had five unlabeled icons.
  • Live location status showing hours, wait time and distance.
  • Upgrade plans in-app, a direct revenue path for the owner.
  • Add to Wallet for Apple, Google and Samsung, so the pass is one double-click away.
  • Liquid glass tab bar and sheets, with light and dark mode.
